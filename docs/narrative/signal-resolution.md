# Narrative Design: The Signal & Resolution

## Narrative Core

*The Last Signal* explores isolation, scientific obsession, and closed recursive loops. Station Echo was established in 1978 in an uninhabited sub-Arctic valley ostensibly to study atmospheric ionospheric anomalies. In truth, the array was locked to an unlisted frequency: 1420.405 MHz — the exact hydrogen line carrier.

---

## Narrative Facts & Progression Tracing

The game tracks nine distinct narrative facts via `NarrativeFactRegistry`:

1. **`SecurityLogRead`**
   - *Source*: Security gatehouse station logbook (`doc-security-log`).
   - *Revelation*: Personnel evacuated abruptly following an unscheduled broadcast surge. The supervisor remained behind in the radio bunker.

2. **`GeneratorRestored`**
   - *Source*: Completing generator startup sequence.
   - *Revelation*: Restoring power wakes dormant automated telemetry systems and triggers the facility's internal carrier relays.

3. **`FirstTransmissionDecoded`**
   - *Source*: Locking and demodulating the primary frequency band.
   - *Revelation*: The transmission contains structured biometric telemetric packets matching human circadian cycles.

4. **`ImpossibleTimestampNoticed`**
   - *Source*: Inspecting the decoded transmission headers.
   - *Revelation*: The signal timestamp matches the exact millisecond the player entered the perimeter gate.

5. **`AntennaAligned`**
   - *Source*: Calibrating the microwave dish array on the gantry rooftop.
   - *Revelation*: Strongest signal reception does not point toward deep space or distant radio towers; it points downward into the facility foundation.

6. **`LocalLoopResultRevealed`**
   - *Source*: Executing source bearing triangulation on the receiver console.
   - *Revelation*: Triangulation confirms zero parsec delay and zero angular offset: the source is a closed local loop operating from within Station Echo's bedrock vault.

7. **`ThreatEncounterSurvived`**
   - *Source*: Evading the manifestation during the aftermath encounter.
   - *Revelation*: The signal is not just audio or radio telemetry; high signal amplification materializes an acoustic-visual entity that hunts based on sound and sight.

8. **`ArchiveEvidenceDiscovered`**
   - *Source*: Inspecting the classified project dossier (`doc-archive-report`) in the supervisor's quarters.
   - *Revelation*: Station Echo was built not to receive a signal, but to contain an infinite feedback loop generated by an automated transmitter that echoes the observer's thoughts.

9. **`FinalTerminalAccessed`**
   - *Source*: Interacting with the Central Command Terminal.
   - *Revelation*: Station command leaves the final resolution to the on-site technician.

## The Three Endings

### Ending 1: Protocol Omega — Total Station Silence (`SILENCE`)
- **Action**: The technician disconnects the carrier bus, discharges the receiver capacitor bank, and pulls the master facility breaker.
- **Tone**: Bleak, resolute, quiet.
- **Narrative Arc**: The entity shrieks as the carrier collapsing starves its physical cohesion. Darkness and freezing Arctic silence reclaim Station Echo. The loop is broken, but humanity will never know what was speaking.

### Ending 2: Protocol Sigma — Broadcast Response (`RESPONSE`)
- **Action**: The technician configures the rooftop microwave dish as a high-gain transmitter and transmits a reverse-phase telemetry ping back into the sky.
- **Tone**: Unsettling, cosmic dread, irreversible.
- **Narrative Arc**: The array hums with immense power, discharging an acknowledgment pulse through the ionosphere. Minutes later, the receiver dials flare green: an echo returns with updated coordinates. Something out there now knows Station Echo is awake.

### Ending 3: Protocol Delta — Offline Archive Extraction (`ARCHIVE`)
- **Action**: The technician engages the magnetic reel storage unit, writing all demodulated carrier data and telemetry headers to a spool of high-grade magnetic tape before severing the receiver.
- **Tone**: Pragmatic, conspiratorial, suspenseful.
- **Narrative Arc**: The physical tape is extracted into a sealed transport container. Station Echo goes dark, but the anomaly has been recorded. The truth leaves the valley in the technician's pocket.
